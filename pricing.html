Frequently Asked Questions

How fresh are the leads?
Every lead is researched and verified within the last 30 days. No stale databases, no recycled lists.
What information is included?
Depends on the plan, but typically: company name, decision maker names, phone, email, industry, revenue estimates, and company size.
Are these leads exclusive?
Yes. Once you purchase a lead list, those specific leads are never sold to anyone else. They're yours alone.
What's your refund policy?
100% satisfaction guaranteed. If leads don't meet quality expectations, we'll provide replacements at no cost.
How do I get the leads?
After purchase, you'll receive a download link immediately. Leads are delivered as a clean CSV file ready to import into your CRM.
Can I customize a list?
Absolutely. Tell me your criteria—industry, location, company size, revenue range—and I'll build exactly what you need.
